# Enclosed and mixed palace ranges

**Living research brief, 2026-09-22.** A range can house storage, rooms, working spaces and circulation in different stretches. Treat its use and access direction as part of the configuration, rather than assuming that every repeated bay is an open walkway.

## Inspected evidence

The official Sajeongjeon description places the 천자고–월자고 stores in the ranges beside Sajeongmun. Lee Hyewon's scholarly account adds rooms, halls and kitchens in the east/west ranges and a northern maru used from Gangnyeongjeon's side. Her chronology distinguishes the 1867 southern range from the east/west ranges rebuilt in 1888 after the 1876 fire. These dates and uses are source statements awaiting detailed plan comparison. See [official description, note-001](../sources/src-khs-sajeongjeon-description/source.json) and [AKS Sajeongjeon, notes 001–002](../sources/src-aks-sajeongjeon/source.json).

Geunjeongjeon's range descriptions similarly distinguish open south circulation, a north range entered from Sajeongjeon's side, and east/west stretches used for rooms, offices and stores. A visually repeated exterior therefore need not imply identical interior use or access. See [AKS Geunjeongjeon, note-001](../sources/src-aks-geunjeongjeon/source.json). This does not identify every surviving bay's present condition.

## Provisional fallback proposals

Begin an **enclosed service/storage configuration** with a complete structural range divided into usable spaces, explicit access sides, floors, partitions, openings, ceilings and a connected roof. Add heating or kitchen construction only where the selected use requires it, using declared assumptions when evidence is absent. Do not infer storage contents or room occupation from a facade alone.

For a **mixed range**, let open and enclosed stretches share compatible support and roof vocabulary while preserving their different boundaries. Gates interrupt both circulation and roof construction and need a complete local assembly. Room modules must be able to vary in span, depth, floor type and opening placement. A missing plan does not authorize a hollow shell: modeling chooses a coherent provisional interior and keeps that choice separate from historical observations.
