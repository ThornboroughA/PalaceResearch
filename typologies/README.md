# Living briefs for Korean palace buildings

These briefs collect the architectural relationships needed to build complete, sensible palace buildings. They begin with Gyeongbokgung examples and will be corrected and extended as research accumulates. **A residence, a working hall and a covered passage need different configurations even when they share timber construction vocabulary.** The types below are practical research groupings, not an exhaustive historical classification.

| Brief | Initial configurations | Strongest current evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Halls](halls.md) | Working hall; monumental ceremonial hall | Sajeongjeon drawings and official description; Guo's Geunjeongjeon/Injeongjeon analysis |
| [Residences](residences.md) | Maru-centred residence with heated rooms and toe spaces | Authored Gangnyeongjeon/Gyotaejeon descriptions; selected excavation-report images |
| [Covered walkways and connectors](covered-walkways.md) | Open circulation stretch; passage joining buildings or ranges | Geunjeongjeon range descriptions; dated 1395 account of connections |
| [Enclosed and mixed ranges](enclosed-ranges.md) | Service/storage range; range combining rooms and circulation | Sajeongjeon and Geunjeongjeon precinct descriptions |

Each brief separates **inspected evidence**, **provisional fallback proposals**, and the **next evidence that would improve it**. The proposals help the later modeling discussion; they are not approved component families or historical measurements. A commissioned model must still be complete: absent evidence calls for an explicit, revisable choice in its modeling brief. It does not authorize an omitted floor, enclosure, support or roof junction.

## Shared vocabulary, with local examples

Use these terms to connect findings across briefs. Similar names do not guarantee identical sizes, details or uses.

| Term | Working meaning and research consequence | Inspected example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 칸; 주칸 | Bay; spacing between posts. Preserve individual spans and measuring basis rather than assuming a fixed length. | Sajeongjeon [obs-006–010](../buildings/gbg-sajeongjeon.json) |
| 기둥; 고주; 가구 | Posts; tall posts; the assembled timber frame. Relate inner supports, outer supports and roof members before assigning room walls. | [Sajeongjeon description, note-002](../sources/src-khs-sajeongjeon-description/source.json); [Guo, note-001](../sources/src-guo-joseon-timber-2005/source.json) |
| 마루; 온돌 | Timber floor space; heated floor/room. Their adjacency and enclosure are as important as their surface material. | [Gangnyeongjeon, note-001](../sources/src-aks-gangnyeongjeon/source.json) |
| 툇마루; 툇간 | Edge timber-floor space; peripheral bay. Record which interior, exterior or adjoining building it connects to. | Gangnyeongjeon and Gyotaejeon, each note-001 |
| 분합문; 교창 | Multi-leaf partition/opening; upper transom opening. Leaf count, framing and operation require separate evidence. | [Sajeongjeon description, note-003](../sources/src-khs-sajeongjeon-description/source.json); Gangnyeongjeon note-001 |
| 우물천장; 연등천장 | Coffered ceiling; ceiling exposing the roof framing/rafters. Roof form does not by itself establish the interior ceiling. | Sajeongjeon note-002; [Geunjeongmun/ranges, note-001](../sources/src-aks-geunjeongmun-ranges/source.json) |
| 행각; 월랑; 천랑 | Range; a term used for a covered range; connecting passage. Check the source's actual enclosure, access side and endpoints. | [Comparison C-003](../COMPARISONS.md#c-003-ranges-and-connectors-are-spatially-different) |
| 기단; 월대 | Building platform; projecting ceremonial platform. Do not copy a named platform onto every related building. | [Comparison C-002](../COMPARISONS.md#c-002-residential-similarity-does-not-erase-variants) |

Bracket terms such as 다포, 이익공 and 초익공 remain attached to their named examples. This first pass does not supply a bracket-family construction recipe, a standard bay, a universal roof pitch or a default timber section.

## How these briefs should improve

For a future “proceed with X building” run, read its existing record and the relevant brief before choosing a bounded research question. Add precise findings to source/building records first; amend only the affected brief paragraph or variant, and record a material contrast in [COMPARISONS.md](../COMPARISONS.md). Keep a useful next question instead of a growing list of speculative subtypes.

When a finding changes a proposed configuration, retain the scope of the example and explain the changed relationship. Modeling records the revision it used and deliberately adopts any update; new research does not silently change earlier models. Review status here means an agent checked a source reading, not that the owner approved a design. Initial edition: **2026-09-22**.
