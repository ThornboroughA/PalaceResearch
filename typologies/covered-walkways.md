# Covered walkways and connectors

**Living research brief, 2026-09-22.** This brief covers open circulation stretches and passages that connect buildings or ranges. “Gallery” and “cloister-like” are useful English descriptions, not exact equivalents for every Korean 행각 or 월랑.

## Inspected evidence

Cho Jaemo describes Geunjeongjeon's south 월랑 as having columns without a courtyard-facing wall. His account gives other sides of the precinct different enclosures and uses. Kim Dong-wook's Geunjeongmun/ranges entry describes the rebuilt range's double arrangement, low platform, round posts, 초익공 brackets, 5량 roof framing and exposed-rafter ceiling. These are descriptions of this example; they do not establish one universal open-gallery section. See [Geunjeongjeon, note-001](../sources/src-aks-geunjeongjeon/source.json) and [Geunjeongmun/ranges, note-001](../sources/src-aks-geunjeongmun-ranges/source.json).

The inspected 1395 palace-completion account distinguishes perimeter 行廊 from connecting 穿廊 and describes a passage's endpoints. This establishes why connections need their own record, but it cannot justify attaching that early passage to an 1867 or current building. The Korean translation and Chinese transcription were checked; the original facsimile was not. See [Taejo Sillok, note-001](../sources/src-sillok-palace-completion-1395/source.json).

## Provisional fallback proposals

An **open circulation stretch** needs a continuous usable route, foundations and surface treatment, posts and roof supports, a complete roof, and a defined treatment of its outer edge. “Open” specifies an actual side; it does not mean removing every boundary. Let adjacent enclosed stretches use the [mixed-range brief](enclosed-ranges.md).

A **connector** additionally needs two explicit destinations, resolved thresholds and levels, and a chosen roof/structure junction at each end. Turns, gates and projecting buildings need local solutions rather than repeated straight bays pushed through an intersection. Missing junction evidence calls for a sensible recorded construction assumption, never a hole between assemblies.

Keep bay width, depth, post form, roof profile, floor level and enclosure independent until a documented example supports tying them together. No numerical default is established here.
