# Cross-building comparisons

No comparative findings have been recorded yet.

Use this file when the collected evidence actually supports a useful relationship. Keep it short and link to building observation IDs or precise source locators; the measurements themselves remain in their authoritative records.

A useful note states **what was compared, which buildings/states it covers, what is shared, what differs, and what remains unverified**. Distinguish a comparison stated by a source from one made by the collecting agent.

General construction findings can be referenced here too, with their documented scope. A pattern seen in two examples is not automatically a rule for all residential halls, periods, or palaces. Differences are useful data, not errors to smooth away.

These notes may later suggest module families or experiments. They do not approve a modeling recipe, prescribe a stylistic compromise, or require that similar historical components use identical geometry.
